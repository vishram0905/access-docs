    Results¶

    All figures are a snapshot as of 12 October 2026. The library grows continuously as verification proceeds.

    What ACCESS can do today¶

    A researcher fills in a short form and immediately sees the programmes they are eligible for. Each "yes" is backed by the funder's own words, results are ranked by fit, deadlines are labelled as open, upcoming or closed, and an application plan or a cited answer to a question is available on request.

    How well each component works¶

    Component What we measured Result
    Harvester filter Share of pages passed on that were real programmes 67% (plus 25% index pages used for links)
    Harvester filter Real programmes wrongly discarded (sample of 60) 10% (95% CI 5–20%)
    Info Critic Agreement between judges (Fleiss' κ, 1,888 judgements) 0.51, moderate
    Info Critic, locate step True claims found on the page: deadline / amount / rule quote 85% / 92% / 98%
    Info Critic, locate step Planted wrong deadlines / amounts that slipped through 0.5% / 0%
    Auditor, panel alone Planted errors caught: invented rule / wrong page / flipped nationality / shifted deadline / amount ×4 100% / 98% / 51% / 27% / 21%
    Claim check (pilot) Flipped nationality rules caught 4 of 4
    Research matcher Recall@5 on 100 known-item queries 0.93 (95% CI 0.88–0.97)
    Eligibility gate Hand-checked unit tests / consistency checks 32 of 32 / 4 of 4
    Q&A Faithful answers 24 of 24 (95% lower bound 0.86)

    What measuring taught us¶

    Measuring each step exposed problems that would otherwise have stayed hidden. Each fix was measured before and after, with a control wherever a change could loosen a check.

    Area Problem found Change Measured effect
    Prompt wording Correct amounts rejected: "pays X" vs a page saying "up to X" Claim accepts a stated maximum 35 of 44 recovered; control: only 1 of 28 wrong amounts accepted
    Data schema "Multiple countries" matched every country filter Explicit, verified list of countries or regions Search for Japan: ~156 → 94 results
    Scheduling Ranking took up to 9 minutes Live requests get priority on model quota 29 of 29 ranked in 55 s; background up to 40% slower
    Whole-record judging Missed most subtle numeric errors Claim-by-claim check Planted wrong amounts and deadlines now caught at the locate step
    Pipeline health Provider outages counted against pages, silently retiring 15,729 queued pages Outages pause work instead Extraction resumed

    Threats to validity¶

    • Sample sizes. Benchmarks use 100 queries and small pilots; confidence intervals are wide, and differences within overlapping intervals are not claimed as significant.
    • Synthetic queries. Benchmark queries are generated from the same text that is indexed, which can favour keyword matching. Results are relative comparisons, not absolute search quality.
    • Coverage bias. The library reflects which sites were reachable and processed first.
    • Hand-set parameters. Trust weights, gap-cost estimates and the 0.7 / 0.3 ranking weights are expert choices, not learned.

    Not yet evaluated¶

    • Detection rates of the full verification process (panel and claim check together), per error type, at scale.
    • Reliability of each judge individually, on a fixed test set (experiment in progress).
    • Ranked eligible results against a relevance-only baseline (objective 4).
